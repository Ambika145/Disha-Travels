Pure Veg & Jain Group Tours. Every meal sorted.

Fixed departures across India, South East Asia, Dubai and Europe where every group meal is vegetarian, from the first hotel breakfast to the last airport meal. Jain meals are arranged on advance request.

A day on a Pure Veg tour

  1. MorningBreakfast, already briefed

    The hotel kitchen has our vegetarian and Jain brief in writing before the group arrives.

  2. MiddayLunch, booked on the route

    A table reserved at a vegetarian restaurant on the way, so no one is hunting for food at 2pm.

  3. EveningDinner, local or Indian

    A local vegetarian meal one night, Indian the next, so nobody eats the same dal for twelve days.
