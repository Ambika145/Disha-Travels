Who We Are

A Hyderabad travel team that plans the holiday, files the visa, books the tickets, and still picks up the phone when you’re halfway across the world.

Disha Travel Services opened in Jawahar Nagar in 2011. Since then, we’ve planned honeymoons in South Africa, family trips across Dubai, group tours through Vietnam and weekends closer to home, along with the passports, visas, forex and insurance that go with them.

We’ve grown, but the way we work hasn’t changed. You won’t be passed between departments or handed to a call centre. The team that plans your trip is the team you call from it: before you fly, while you’re away, and after you’re home.
