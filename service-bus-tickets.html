Choose your coach.

The right coach depends on how far you’re going and when.

AC Sleeper

Best forLong overnight routes

Flat berths in an air-conditioned coach. The most comfortable way to cover a long distance overnight.

Multi-axle AC Seater

Best forLonger day or night journeys

Smoother ride, reclining seats and more luggage space.

Non-AC Sleeper

Best forCooler months, shorter nights

Flat berths at a lower fare, best when the weather is mild.

Comfortable for journeys of a few hours between towns.
