Visa Services in Hyderabad

Six things that get visa files sent back.

We check every file for these before it goes anywhere near the embassy.

Bank statements that don’t match the trip

A sudden large deposit, or a balance that looks too thin for the trip, raises questions.

Names spelt differently

The passport, the ticket and the hotel booking must all show your name exactly the same way.

A missing leave letter or ITR

Proof that you have a job or business to come back to is one of the first things an officer looks for.

Insurance that falls short

Schengen visas need at least €30,000 of medical cover for the whole stay.

A vague itinerary

Hotels, dates and the order of countries should tell one clear, consistent story.

Applying to the wrong country

For a multi-country Schengen trip, you apply to the country where you spend the most nights.
