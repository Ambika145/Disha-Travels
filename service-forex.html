Cash or a travel card?

Most trips need a bit of both. Here’s how they compare.

Cash

Best for
Taxis, tips, markets and the first day after you land
If it’s lost
It’s gone
Limit
Up to USD 3,000 in cash per trip for most countries
Leftover
Can be sold back or kept for the next trip

Travel card

Best for
Hotels, shopping, restaurants and ATMs
If it’s lost
Block it and get a replacement
Currencies
Several currencies loaded on one card
Leftover
Stays on the card, or can be refunded
