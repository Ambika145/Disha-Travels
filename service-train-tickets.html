Which class suits your trip?

For elders, we request lower berths from the quota set aside for senior citizens.

1A

First AC

Private cabins with lockable doors. The most comfortable way to travel overnight.

2A

AC 2-tier

Curtained berths, quieter coaches. A popular choice for families.

3A

AC 3-tier

The most booked AC class, with good value on long routes.

3E

AC 3-tier Economy

Like 3A with slightly more berths per coach.

SL

Sleeper

Non-AC berths. Best for short overnight hops in cooler months.

CC

AC Chair Car

Reserved seats on day trains, including Shatabdi and Vande Bharat.

EC

Executive Chair Car

Wider seats and more space on premium day trains.

2S

Second Sitting

Reserved non-AC seats on short day journeys.

Reading Your Ticket

CNF, RAC or WL: what it means.

If a train is waitlisted, we tell you the realistic chances and plan a backup.

CNF

Confirmed

Your berth is confirmed. The coach and berth number are on the ticket or the chart.

RAC

Reservation Against Cancellation

You can board and share a side berth. It may upgrade to a full berth if others cancel.

WL

Waitlisted

Not confirmed yet. An e-ticket still waitlisted when the chart is made is cancelled and refunded automatically.
